One publishing house, with books and audio built as coherent editions rather than disconnected products.

Copy Real is the house.

Copy Real is an independent publishing house for original novels, illustrated editions, territory-specific classics, children’s publishing, research-led work and audiobook editions.

Copy Real Audio is the audiobook division of Copy Real. AudioVerse is the legacy name that preceded it and is being phased out through normal edition updates rather than an artificial overnight rebrand.
