ABOUT THE
ALMANAC

The A.I. Almanac is a long-form annual record of artificial intelligence and the social, economic, institutional and personal changes occurring around it.

AI

Published by Copy Real as an annual series.

Why an almanac?

Artificial intelligence is usually discussed as technology news: new models, benchmarks, products and predictions. The Almanac takes a wider view. It asks what those changes are doing to ordinary human life and how those effects interact with pressures that are already present.

The annual format creates a fixed point in time. Each edition can record what looked important then, what evidence was available, what people expected next and which assumptions later proved wrong.

What it covers

The recurring subject areas include AI capability and deployment, employment, automation, money, housing, cost of living, education, fertility, family formation, relationships, loneliness, health, ageing, religion, meaning, social cohesion, information environments and governance.

How the series develops

The 2026 edition establishes the baseline, predominantly through British and Western evidence. The 2027 edition deliberately widens the comparison through China and East Asia. The 2028 edition is intended to move towards a fully world-conscious structure in which major subjects are examined across the world systems and regions most relevant to the question.

Publisher

The A.I. Almanac is published by Copy Real. The wider Copy Real catalogue includes original fiction, non-fiction, illustrated editions, restored classics and Copy Real Audio productions.
