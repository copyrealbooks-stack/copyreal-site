The baseline edition is predominantly British and Western in its evidence base. That is treated as a starting point, not a universal model.

What the first edition does

The 2026 Almanac creates a structured record of the forces already reshaping ordinary life: artificial intelligence, automation, employment, cost of living, housing, education, relationships, health, information and social confidence.

Its role in the series is architectural. Later editions can only show what has changed if the first edition records where the series began.

Why a baseline matters

Fast-moving technological change creates a memory problem. A development that feels extraordinary in one year can feel normal very quickly. The annual format is designed to preserve those transitions rather than flatten them into hindsight.

Where are we?

That is the governing question of the 2026 edition. The 2027 edition then asks whether the same map still holds when viewed through other major social, economic and institutional systems.
