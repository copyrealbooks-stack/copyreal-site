Research standard

Comparison without dilution.

The Almanac is not built by collecting isolated statistics. It looks for converging evidence, disagreement between sources, differences between systems and the human consequences behind the numbers.

Primary first

National statistical and regulatory sources are used wherever possible, then cross-checked against international bodies, peer-reviewed research and credible independent analysis.

Triangulate

No country's official statistics are automatically dismissed or treated as a complete interpretation. Claims are tested against other evidence.

Depth before breadth

One deeply researched comparison is preferable to six superficial national statistics. International evidence is included only when it adds explanatory power.
