Publishing rule

Territory first.

A title can be open in one market and protected in another. Copy Real's catalogue architecture therefore separates the book record from the territories in which a particular edition can actually be offered.

Print

Edition artwork, interior material and publication metadata are controlled independently.

Audio

Copy Real Audio editions can have separate Pure Narration and CFX versions, with their own distribution status.

Retail destinations should only appear where the underlying rights position has been confirmed.
