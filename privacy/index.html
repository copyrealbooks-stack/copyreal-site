Privacy notice

Last updated 21 November 2026

Copy Real provides publishing information, software tools and individually arranged editorial and production services. This notice explains what happens when you use our website or contact us. For privacy enquiries, email copyrealbooks@gmail.com.

Browser-only tools

The Creator Studio's free text tools and the Toolbox's local image and diagnostic tools are designed to process what you enter within your own browser, without submitting the manuscript text or selected artwork to Copy Real's server. In particular, the KDP cover builder renders artwork locally. A tool that opens an email draft does not send that email unless you choose to send it in your email application.

Your device, browser extensions and third-party services you choose to copy content into are outside Copy Real's control. Please avoid putting passwords, account identifiers or sensitive unpublished manuscripts into initial enquiry forms.

Emails and quotations

When you choose to email us, we receive the information you include, such as your name, email address, book title, word count, publishing brief and requested services. We use it to reply, assess work, prepare quotations and maintain records of any agreed project. Please do not send full manuscripts until we have agreed a suitable private transfer route.

Payments and services

Our £39 Book Title & Positioning Pack and £79 Audiobook Feasibility Review use Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe processes the payment and card information. We may receive order details, contact details and the project information entered at checkout so we can fulfil the service. We do not collect card numbers on Copy Real's static website. Stripe has its own privacy notice.

Hosting, advertising and third-party services

The website is served using Cloudflare Pages, whose systems may retain technical access and security data. The home page currently contains Google's advertising script; whether advertising is served depends on the applicable Google account and site approval. Advertising technologies may involve cookies or similar identifiers subject to Google's policies and applicable browser choices. Other external websites, retailers and payment providers linked from Copy Real have their own practices. See Google's privacy policy for more information.

Retention and requests

We retain customer correspondence and work records as needed to respond to enquiries, supply contracted services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes and comply with applicable obligations. We do not promise a universal fixed deletion period for all records. You may contact us at copyrealbooks@gmail.com to ask about information we hold about you, or request correction or deletion where applicable. We may need to retain some records for legal or accounting reasons.

Children and external platforms

Our editorial and publishing services are intended for adults or authorised representatives of a child author. Do not submit a child's personal details through a public contact form without appropriate permission. Links to KDP, Stripe, Google and other third-party services lead away from this site.

Changes

We will update this notice when our tools, hosting, payment methods or data-processing arrangements materially change. For questions about a particular project, email the Copy Real business inbox.

This notice describes the present site; it is not a certification of compliance with every applicable privacy or advertising regulation.
