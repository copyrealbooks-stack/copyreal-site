How the calculation works

We divide manuscript words by the number of words narrated per minute to estimate finished running time. We then multiply that time by your all-in cost per finished hour and add any one-off costs.

The break-even figure divides total costs by your assumed net income per sale, rounded up to the next whole sale.

What to include in your assumptions

  • Narration, editing, proof-listening, mastering and revisions.
  • Cover artwork, distribution fees and launch costs.
  • Your own time, even if you are doing the production yourself.
  • A realistic net amount received per sale — not the retail price.
