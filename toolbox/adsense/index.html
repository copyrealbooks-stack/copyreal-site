Before you request another review

  1. Identify whether the issue concerns the website, your AdSense account or AdSense for YouTube. These are different review routes.
  2. Use the correct existing account. Creating another application can worsen a duplicate-account problem.
  3. Check the exact notices in AdSense and Google email rather than relying on a guessed cause.
  4. Confirm accessible original content, navigation, clear site ownership and any required privacy disclosures.
  5. For ads.txt, publish only your authorised publisher ID and the correct Google seller line, if applicable.
  6. Keep screenshots and dates of rejection messages before re-submitting or contacting official support.
