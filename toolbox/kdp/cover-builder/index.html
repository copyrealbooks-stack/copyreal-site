Paperback Cover Wrap PDF Maker

Convert your own complete front–spine–back cover artwork into a PDF at the physical dimensions required by your KDP paperback template. Nothing is uploaded; the image is processed in your browser.

Read this first. This tool creates an accurately sized candidate PDF; it does not design a cover, repair low-resolution images, create barcode clearance, validate ISBN metadata or certify KDP acceptance. Use the exact spine measurement and check every edge against the official KDP template and KDP Print Previewer. Paperback only. Hardcover hinge and case-laminate dimensions differ.

1. Enter your official paperback measurements

Fixed at 0.125 inches for this paperback tool.
Enter the official spine width to calculate your cover's physical dimensions.

2. Add your complete wrap artwork

Your image should already contain the back cover on the left, the spine in the centre, and the front cover on the right, including the outside bleed. This tool does not assemble three separate images.

No manuscript, artwork or file name is transmitted to Copy Real.

If the source image has the wrong aspect ratio, filling will crop it; fitting the whole image may leave empty margins. Neither option can reconstruct missing bleed or reposition text. For critical print work, adjust the artwork to the actual KDP template first.

Dashed red lines in the preview indicate approximate vertical panel and trim guides. They are excluded from the exported PDF. Artwork, including any text near the spine or barcode area, must be checked manually.

3. Review before uploading

  • Confirm format, paper and page count against the exact KDP cover template used to obtain the spine width.
  • Inspect whether the existing full-wrap design contains all required elements and actual bleed artwork.
  • Check the exported PDF page size against the official template and inspect its spine and panel alignment.
  • Check the back-cover barcode exclusion area; do not draw a placeholder barcode unless instructed by your chosen publisher.
  • Upload the PDF to KDP Print Previewer, inspect every warning and order a proof when appropriate.

This utility exports a single-page, RGB JPEG-image PDF. Text is rasterised: if your design needs crisp vector typography, colour-managed print or a non-standard cover specification, use professional layout software or request assistance.
