# Copy Real Audio — cover production brief

Date: 27 September 2026. Source: user-supplied All Book Covers folder and existing website covers. This is a production specification, **not** a claim that rebranded images have been created.

## Brand templates

- Master: 3000×3000 sRGB JPG or PNG. Use source art at sufficient resolution; do not enlarge thumbnail files as though they were high-resolution masters.
- Pure Narration: original artwork, COPY REAL AUDIO branding, a legible `PURE NARRATION` identifier and established gold microphone identity. No legacy AV mark.
- CFX: coordinated artwork with COPY REAL CFX and `CINEMATIC EDITION` identifier; visually distinct from PN at thumbnail size. No old brand. CFX editions use sound design without added music.
- Preserve approved art. Do not manufacture a corresponding edition merely because one edition's image exists.
- Exact title/author/narrator text must be verified; **Mondaloy** is canonical, not `Mondaloy` or `Mondaloy`.

## Queue based on identifiable assets, not on assumed audio completion

1. The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe: confirm supplied image's edition; retain approved distinct PN/CFX designs, check both squares and legibility.
2. The War of the Worlds (H. G. Wells), The Time Machine and Frankenstein: retrieve existing approved edition artwork first; do not regenerate unnecessarily.
3. Murder at the Vicarage: inspect latest JPG and compare against published PN artwork, prepare Copy Real branded version only if legacy-marked.
4. The Coming Race: review existing PN image. No CFX implied.
5. Prince Caspian: review both PN and CFX JPG/PNG pairs, remove old branding only where present; verify territorial permissions and product status.
6. Crimea, What Is Happening Now? and Isan Bedtime Stories: inspect artwork, confirm actual edition, then determine rebranding requirements.
7. Mondaloy: use old `monaloy-audio.jpg` and any approved book artwork as visual reference. Plan consistent new branding and correct printed title; retain legacy file/URL aliases until tested.
8. Apocalypse Park, Trion: Ascension, Glitch, The Last Exodus, Free Party: reference art only until the corresponding audiobook production is verified complete.

## Review checklist

- Visually identify unnamed Drive files before matching any title.
- Compare source covers to avoid duplicates and preserve user-approved art.
- Confirm correct title, author, narrator, rights, edition type, graphic legibility, dimensions and absence of old marks on new art.
- Export matching PN/CFX filenames without overwriting originals; retain audit provenance.
- Keep checkout off, store development-only and R2 private pending signed-off audio assets and delivery tests.
