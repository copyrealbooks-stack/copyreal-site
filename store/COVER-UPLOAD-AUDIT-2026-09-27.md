## Production policy
1. Preserve original uploaded artwork and AV-era covers as source material; create separately named Copy Real PN/CFX exports only after visual review.
2. Prefer completed audiobook editions first, but do not infer completion from cover files or repository thumbnails.
3. Confirm exact title, author, edition, year, existing typography and territorial permissions before publishing new artwork. Do not confuse H. G. Wells's The War of the Worlds with the original Ash and Iron trilogy.
4. Site eBook/thumbnail references are in `assets/covers/` and `assets/covers/catalogue/`; legacy audio references are in `assets/covers/audio/`. These can guide style but should not be represented as approved PN/CFX pairs without review.
5. New artwork targets: square 3000 x 3000 px masters with clearly different Pure Narration versus CFX treatment; do not replace existing approved finished designs unnecessarily.
6. Keep all store SKUs disabled pending actual verified audio samples, rights, prices, payments and private delivery tests.
