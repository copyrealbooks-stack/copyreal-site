01 / Idea development

Book Title Lab

Describe the book in your own words. This is a free, template-assisted brainstorming engine; it is not a generative AI model, a title-availability search or a trade mark check.

Expect a few rough options. The point is to find promising angles, then refine them. These suggestions have not been screened against existing books or rights.

02 / Describe and position your book

Book Brief Builder

Before you ask anyone to write a blurb, assemble the facts that make your story, subject or argument worth reading.

This produces an editorial brief, not invented marketing copy. A short, optional AI drafting instruction is included so you can develop the work elsewhere.
