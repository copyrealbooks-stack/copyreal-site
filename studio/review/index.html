Review My Book

Want to know whether your manuscript works before you spend money publishing it? We can scope a candid editorial assessment. That means reading the actual work, identifying its strengths and defects, and explaining what to revise — rather than returning a flattering AI-generated summary.

This is editorial feedback for the author. It isn't a public consumer review, paid star rating or promise of endorsement.

Choose the level of review

Smaller first step

Opening Chapter Critique

Review the opening's clarity, reader expectations, pacing, viewpoint, prose and immediate weaknesses. Scope depends on actual chapter length. Quote provided before payment.

Whole-book diagnosis

Structural Manuscript Review

Assess the overall architecture: chronology, continuity, narrative propulsion, character agency, repetition, chapter boundaries and unresolved threads.

Non-fiction

Argument & Evidence Review

Examine organisation, overstatement, source support, distinction between evidence and inference, and gaps requiring research. A full source audit is separately scoped.

Important boundaries: A review reports what was checked, with examples and unresolved findings. It does not automatically include rewriting, line editing, proof correction, legal clearance, fact-checking every claim or preparing KDP files. Those can be quoted separately.
