Problems worth fixing

Fiction

Character and scene drift

Viewpoint inconsistencies, generic action, repeating metaphors, implausible dialogue, weak escalation or chapters that forget what the novel is supposed to be doing.

Non-fiction

Evidence and assertion

Confident claims without sources, stale statistics, muddled chronology, circular argument and the failure to separate observation from inference.

Any draft

Voice and repetition

The same sentence shapes, stock formulations, tonal inconsistency, inflated transitions and prose that never develops a distinct voice.

How a paid assessment works

1. Establish the brief

You provide the intended reader, genre, sample length, current status and specific concerns. Identify passages that are already approved and must remain untouched.

2. Diagnose before rewriting

We identify examples, contradictions and changes required. Factual matters are checked against suitable sources within an agreed scope; unsupported claims remain flagged until verified.

3. Agree the work

We quote separately for a structural report, substantive rewrite, line edit, continuity audit or source audit. Pricing depends on length and complexity. No manuscript is altered without a defined scope.

What we will not promise
  • That edited work will be classified as “human-written” by any detector.
  • That a publisher, university or platform will accept undisclosed AI use.
  • That changing words alone resolves plagiarism, copyright or factual problems.
  • That a full book can be responsibly assessed without reading it.

Use of AI must be disclosed where required by your publisher, institution or distribution platform. Rights and permissions remain the responsibility of the relevant author or rights holder.
