Writer's Block Rescue

You know your book better than a machine does. This tool helps you identify the next decision and start writing again. It generates a structured set of exercises in your browser; it doesn't read or analyse a whole manuscript.

Two or three sentences are enough. Do not paste sensitive unpublished manuscript passages.

A tool, not a ghostwriter.

These prompts are deliberately designed to provoke decisions without writing your novel for you. A stronger manuscript still needs reading, revision and editorial judgement.

For help identifying recurring structural problems, see Review My Book.

Three useful ways out of a difficult passage

Start with consequence

Write the next consequence of what has already happened. You can work backwards to the transition later.

Ask what's missing

Look for an unmade decision, unanswered objection or piece of evidence that could change the situation.

Give it a limit

Write for ten minutes without polishing. Mark uncertain facts in square brackets and keep moving.
