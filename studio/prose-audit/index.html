Manuscript Repetition Scanner

Catch the repetitions that become invisible after your fiftieth read-through. This local tool flags repeated paragraphs, familiar phrase patterns and recurring sentence openings. It cannot judge whether repetition is intentional or effective: that's still an editorial decision.

Maximum 180,000 characters. Long books can be checked chapter by chapter. Do not use this on material you do not have permission to analyse.

Counts are descriptive, not evidence that a word must be removed.

Privacy and scope: this scanner processes text within the browser. It does not upload text to a server, save it between visits, or send it to a model. Browser extensions and the device itself are outside our control. It does not establish whether something was generated by AI.
