How we work

Assisted self-publishing

In the standard service model, the author retains their copyright, chooses the publishing accounts and approves the final files. Copy Real provides the production work stated in the agreement.

Publishing under Copy Real

Imprint consideration, licensing, royalty-sharing, or distribution through Copy Real require separate editorial acceptance and written terms. Purchasing a production service does not guarantee publication under the imprint.
