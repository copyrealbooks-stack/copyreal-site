The King's Road

William J. Dyson
KindlePaperback

England, 1642. Thomas Sexton is young, privileged and hungry for adventure when a reckless night on the King’s Road pulls him into a country at war. What follows is a grounded journey through divided loyalties, hard campaigning and the brutal realities of pike-and-musket warfare, from Newark to Naseby, as Thomas is forced to decide what kind of man he will become when the fighting is over.

Built on decades of research and first-hand involvement in English Civil War re-enactment, The King’s Road combines battlefield detail with the intimate consequences of war for family, duty and home.
