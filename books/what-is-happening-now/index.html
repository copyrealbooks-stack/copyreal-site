The A.I. Almanac · navigation guide to contemporary life

What Is Happening Now?

Chas Burns
eBookPaperbackAudiobook / Copy Real Audio

This is not another book about A.I. It is a practical guide to navigating a world changing at extraordinary speed: work, money, housing, education, relationships, health, information, technology and the decisions that shape an ordinary life. A.I. is used as part of the research and analysis method to help make that world easier to understand.
