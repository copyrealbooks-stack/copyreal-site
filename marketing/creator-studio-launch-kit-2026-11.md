## How to post without wasting the audience's patience

- Post from an authorised Copy Real account; do not claim testimonials, ratings or success stories that do not exist.
- Share one genuinely useful free tool first. Paid help appears as a transparent option, not a surprise.
- Show a practical screen recording of how to paste an error message or use the cost calculator **after checking the public deployment**.
- In author communities, follow the group's promotional rules. If promotion isn't allowed, answer real questions without pitching.
- Avoid mass cold-emailing scraped addresses, auto-comments or pretending to be a satisfied customer.
- Respond to real enquiries with the exact service scope and a useful first diagnostic question.
- Follow up with existing contacts only where there is a genuine relationship and a clear reason to believe they'd welcome it.
- Keep posts varied; do not publish every message on the same day.
