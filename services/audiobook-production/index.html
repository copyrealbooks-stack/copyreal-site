Questions authors ask before commissioning audio

How long will my audiobook be?

Length depends on the final manuscript and performance speed. Use the free calculator to model several plausible words-per-minute assumptions rather than trusting a single fixed conversion.

Can I use an AI voice?

Possibly, but distribution platforms differ in their policies and labelling requirements, and you must have the necessary rights and permissions. We would establish the intended outlets before choosing a production route.

Will Copy Real distribute it for me?

Distribution arrangements can be discussed separately and depend on the actual project, selected platforms and current eligibility. A feasibility review does not include a distribution contract.

What if I already have audio recordings?

We can quote for a targeted technical or editorial assessment based on sample files. The full job depends on format, quality, recording consistency and the final delivery specification.

Want a production quotation rather than a planning review?

Send a short description with book length, languages, preferred narrator, existing audio (if any) and your intended distribution platforms. Do not attach the complete manuscript to an initial enquiry.
