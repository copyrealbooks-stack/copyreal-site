Service information and order terms

Last updated 23 November 2026

Copy Real offers a mixture of free browser tools, fixed-price personal reviews and individually quoted publishing services. Contact us at copyrealbooks@gmail.com before ordering if you need a specific delivery deadline or are uncertain whether a service meets your needs.

$7.99 USD Book Title & Positioning Pack

One book concept: three tailored title suggestions and brief positioning notes. One written response; no revisions included.

$9.99 USD Audiobook Feasibility Review

One title: a concise written estimate of running time, two production routes, an indicative budget and an illustrative break-even calculation. No recording, editing or revisions included.

$9.99 USD KDP Upload Rescue diagnosis

One KDP rejection message, one concise personalised written assessment and practical next-step checklist. No editing of source files, upload to KDP, guarantee of approval or revisions included.

Optional $0.99 USD audio-tool support and $3.99 USD five-tool bundle

These are voluntary support purchases. The five audio utilities and the wider starter archive are already available free from the Toolbox. Paying does not unlock exclusive files, a licence unavailable to free users, technical support or a subscription. Stripe displays a download address after payment. Test all beta tools on disposable copies and inspect their source.

Optional $2.99 USD support contribution

A voluntary contribution to support the free Toolbox. It is not a purchase of software, a subscription or a promise of additional services.

After you pay

Stripe-hosted checkout collects your contact email and a few details about the project. Copy Real will contact you through that address to obtain the information needed for the review and agree delivery. These are personalised services, not instant digital downloads. If you require delivery by a particular date, please ask us before buying rather than assume a deadline.

The service is prepared from your submitted material and appropriate professional judgement. We will identify assumptions or information gaps rather than invent facts. Any expansion of scope, extra revisions or additional services requires separate agreement.

Publishing, KDP repairs and manuscript assessments

Requests for ebook, paperback, hardback, audiobook, KDP repairs and substantive manuscript reviews are individually assessed and quoted. The free online brief builders create an enquiry draft and do not place a paid order. We will agree scope, deliverables, responsibilities, timetable, fee and a suitable way of exchanging source files before commencing any such commission. A quotation does not guarantee publication under the Copy Real imprint or distribution through a particular third-party platform.

Rights and customer material

Authors should only submit text, images, recordings and other material they have the necessary rights to use. Authors retain their pre-existing rights in their own material. Any ownership or licensing of newly commissioned deliverables, and any publishing or distribution rights, should be stated in the individual written agreement. Copy Real will not publish your manuscript under its imprint merely because you bought an editorial or production service.

Cancellations, problems and refunds

If you have ordered the wrong service, cannot provide the required book information or believe the advertised work cannot be supplied, contact copyrealbooks@gmail.com as soon as possible with the order reference. We will assess cancellation and refund requests in light of the work already undertaken, the agreement and applicable consumer law. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Free preparation tools

The cover maker, title lab, KDP decoder and other free utilities are aids for checking and planning. They are not warranties of publication acceptance, title uniqueness, originality, legal compliance or financial results. Always retain source files and inspect generated outputs before submitting them to a publisher or retailer.

Queries and corrections

We aim to correct identifiable defects within the agreed deliverable and clearly distinguish established facts from assumptions. The appropriate response to an issue depends on its scope and cause. Contact the business inbox with the project title and a concise description of the concern.

Services and prices displayed at checkout take precedence over informal discussions. Separate platform conditions, including KDP and audiobook distributor rules, may also apply.
