Common questions

Can you guarantee Amazon will accept my book?

No. Amazon makes the acceptance decision. The scope of a repair covers the specific agreed defects and checks; KDP may identify further issues when a new file is uploaded.

Do I need the original design file?

Editable artwork is best. A high-resolution full-wrap PNG or JPG may be enough for a measured PDF, but some design faults cannot be corrected cleanly from a flattened image. Supply a list of available formats in your enquiry.

Is paperback the same as hardcover?

No. Hardcover case laminate and dust-jacket layouts have different physical requirements. Use the precise template for the format being produced.

Can the free tool fix a blurry cover?

No. Re-encoding or enlarging an image does not recreate missing detail. Start with the best-resolution artwork or request a quote for redesigning affected elements.

Do you need my Amazon password?

No. The standard arrangement is to provide properly prepared files for you to upload and approve yourself. Never send passwords in an enquiry.

Ready for a quote?

Include the format, exact warning message and whether the source is DOCX, PDF, EPUB or a design file. Our first reply will establish what needs inspecting and how any private files should be transferred.
